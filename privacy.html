Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026. Second Ring is a service of Interregnum Ventures LLC, 91 Price Street Fl 2, Kingston, PA 18704.

What we collect

  • From shops (our customers): business name, contact name, email, mobile numbers for the owner and on-call techs, hours, service area and services.
  • From callers to a shop: the phone number that called, and what the caller says on the call, converted to text so we can pass the message to the shop.

Call recording and consent

Pennsylvania requires the consent of everyone on a call before it is recorded. Every call answered by Second Ring opens by saying it is an automated assistant and that the call may be recorded. Audio is used only to create a text transcript and is deleted after transcription. Transcripts are kept up to 90 days so the shop can follow up, then deleted.

Text messages

Second Ring sends operational text alerts only to the shop owner and the shop's on-call technicians who opted in. We do not text callers. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Sharing

Mobile numbers and opt-in data are never sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. We share call details only with the shop the caller was trying to reach, and with service providers that run our phone and payment systems (for example Twilio and Stripe) under their own privacy terms.

Security and AI

Calls are processed on our own computers wherever possible. The assistant never quotes prices or diagnoses problems, and emergency calls get a fixed safety script and are sent to a person.

Your choices

To see or delete your data, write to Interregnum Ventures LLC at the address above. Shops can cancel at any time, and we delete their data within 30 days.
